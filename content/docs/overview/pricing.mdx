## How it works

1. Sign up and use ClawLink free for 7 days. Your first app is included — no card required.
2. Connecting a second app during the trial starts checkout; billing begins only after your trial ends.
3. After the trial, pick **Starter** or **Ultra Heavy** to keep your integrations active. Upgrade anytime from the dashboard.

<Note>
  Upgrade and manage your subscription from **Dashboard > Billing**. Payments are handled by Polar.
</Note>

## Fair-use tool calls

- **Trial** and **Starter** include 1,000 successful tool executions per period (Starter resets each calendar month).
- **Ultra Heavy** includes 100,000 successful tool executions per calendar month.

Tool-call usage is visible in **Dashboard > Billing** so you always know where you stand.
