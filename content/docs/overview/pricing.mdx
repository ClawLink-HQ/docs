## Tool calls

| Plan | Successful tool calls |
|---|---|
| Free trial | 1,000 over the 7 days |
| Starter | 1,000 per calendar month |
| Ultra Heavy | 100,000 per calendar month |

Only successful calls count. You can see your usage at any time in **Dashboard > Billing**.

## Data credit

[Public data apps](/integrations/public-data) (TikTok, Reddit, SEO, crypto, stocks and more) are paid per call from a separate data credit, not from your tool calls. Starter includes $1 and Ultra Heavy includes $5 of data credit every month. You can top up with a $20, $50 or $100 pack.

See [Billing](/billing) for the details.
