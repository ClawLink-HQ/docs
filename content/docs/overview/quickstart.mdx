---
title: "Quick Start"
description: "Install ClawLink, pair your client, and connect your first app in a few minutes"
icon: "Rocket"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up at claw-link.dev">
    Go to [claw-link.dev](https://claw-link.dev) and create your account. New accounts start with a 7-day free trial. Your first app is included, and you do not need a card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your agent">
    ClawLink works three ways: the CLI for any agent, the Hermes plugin, or the OpenClaw plugin. Pick one:

    <SetupTabs app="your first app" />

    Full details on each path are in [Install ClawLink](/install).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your first app">
    In the ClawLink dashboard, open **Connections** and click **Add connection**. Pick the app and complete the hosted flow.

    Good first apps, because setup is quick and the results are easy to check:

    <LogoRow apps="gmail slack google-calendar notion github" labels />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your agent to use it">
    Try prompts like these:

    <Prompts
      items={[
        ["gmail", "Send an email to sarah@example.com with subject 'Hello'"],
        ["slack", "Post a message to #general in Slack saying the deploy is done"],
        ["github", "Create a GitHub issue titled 'Fix login bug' in my main repo"],
        ["google-sheets", "Add a row to my sales tracker Google Sheet"],
        ["stripe", "List my last 5 Stripe customers"],
      ]}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To check the setup, ask your agent: "Using ClawLink, list the apps I have connected." Every call then shows in **Dashboard > Logs**.
</Tip>

<Note>
  API keys in **Settings > Developer** are for agents that cannot run the login command. Browser pairing is the recommended setup. To call ClawLink directly over HTTP with a key, see the [Developer API](/api/overview).
</Note>
