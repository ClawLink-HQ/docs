**Connections** is where you let ClawLink act for you in other apps, and where you manage each connected account. You can connect more than one account of the same app. Each account is its own connection, with its own stable id, and one of them is the **default** for that app.

To add a second account of an app you already use, open that app and click **Connect another account**.

## Connection statuses

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Connected** | The app is authorized and working | Nothing. It is ready to use |
| **Needs reconnect** | The credential expired, was revoked, or needs new consent | Open the connection and click **Reconnect** |
| **Coming soon** | The app is in the catalog, but its connect flow is not live yet | Use **Request an app** to show interest |
| **Included** | A [public data app](/integrations/public-data). Nothing to connect | Use it. Each call is paid from your data credit |

<Warning>
  When a provider token expires, ClawLink keeps the connection but marks it **Needs reconnect**. Tool calls to that app fail until you reconnect.
</Warning>

## More than one account per app

- Connect as many accounts of the same app as you need, for example a work and a personal Gmail.
- One connection is the default. ClawLink uses it when the request does not name a connection.
- Removing a connection removes only that account, never the other accounts of the app.
