The dashboard at [claw-link.dev/dashboard](https://claw-link.dev/dashboard) is where you install ClawLink, connect apps, see what your agent did, and manage billing.

## Sidebar

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="plug" href="/dashboard/integrations">
    Your apps. Add, reconnect and remove connections, and browse the catalog.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Install" icon="rocket" href="/dashboard/install">
    The current setup command for your agent, with a copy button.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Devices" icon="cpu" href="/dashboard/devices">
    Every machine paired to your account. Rename or revoke one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Logs" icon="scroll-text" href="/dashboard/request-logs">
    Every tool call your agent made, with its input, output and errors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Playground" icon="flask-conical" href="/dashboard/playground">
    Try your connected apps in plain language, right in the browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="receipt" href="/billing">
    Your plan, tool-call usage, data credit and invoices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="settings" href="/dashboard/settings">
    Profile, Developer (API keys and MCP setup) and Security.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Feedback" icon="message-square">
    In the account menu. Send a bug report or an idea to the team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Above the menu, the sidebar shows your **Data credit** balance. It pays for [public data apps](/integrations/public-data). Click it to top up.

## Connections home

**Connections** is the first page you see. It shows:

- **Your connections**: the apps you have connected, each with its status.
- **Included with ClawLink**: the public data apps every account has, with nothing to connect.
- The full catalog, with a search box. If an app is missing, click **Request an app**.

<Tip>
  A brand-new account opens on **Install** instead, so you can set up your agent first.
</Tip>
