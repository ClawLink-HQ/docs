**Devices** lists every machine paired to your ClawLink account: each OpenClaw, Hermes or CLI install. A device shows up here when you approve it in your browser.

<LogoRow apps="openclaw hermes claude cursor codex" />

For each device you can see where it came from (OpenClaw plugin, Hermes or CLI), when it was paired and when it was last used.

## Rename a device

Click **Rename** to give a device a clear name, like "Work laptop" or "Home server". This helps when you have more than one install.

## Revoke a device

Click **Revoke** on any device you do not recognize, or no longer use. Its ClawLink credential stops working at once, and that agent can no longer call your apps. Your connections are not changed.

<Tip>
  To use that machine again, pair it again: see [Install ClawLink](/install).
</Tip>

Every call a device makes shows in [Logs](/dashboard/request-logs), with the device in the call details.
