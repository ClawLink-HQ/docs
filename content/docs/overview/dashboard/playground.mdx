The **Playground** lets you try your connected apps in plain language, in the browser. You do not need an agent, a setup or any code. It is a quick way to check that a connection works, or to see what an app can do.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an app">
    The Playground runs real tasks on the apps you have connected. Connect one first in **Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mention the app and ask">
    Type `@` to pick an app, then say what you want. For example: `@gmail summarize my unread email`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve changes">
    Before anything that changes data, like sending an email or creating an issue, the Playground asks **Approve this action?** Nothing runs on your real account until you approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each answer has a **What it did** list, so you can see every tool call it made. Your chats are kept under **Chats**, and **New chat** starts over.

<Note>
  The Playground has its own limit per plan, separate from your monthly tool calls. If you send messages too quickly, wait a moment and try again.
</Note>
