ClawLink connects your AI agent to the apps you use. Install it once, approve the device in your browser, and connect apps from the dashboard. ClawLink handles sign-in, credential storage, retries and request logs for you.

It works with the agent you already use:

<LogoRow apps="openclaw hermes claude cursor codex grokbot muse chatgpt" labels />

## Get started in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up at claw-link.dev">
    Create your account at [claw-link.dev](https://claw-link.dev). New accounts start with a 7-day free trial. Your first app is included, and you do not need a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install ClawLink in your agent">
    Run this in your terminal, or paste it wherever you chat with your agent:

    ```bash
    npx -y @useclawlink/cli login
    ```

    The command prints a link. Open it and approve the device. OpenClaw and Hermes also have native plugins: see [OpenClaw setup](/openclaw) and [Hermes setup](/hermes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your first app">
    Open **Dashboard > Connections**, click **Add connection**, and finish the hosted flow for the app you want. Then ask your agent to use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
