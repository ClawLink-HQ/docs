New accounts start with a **7-day free trial**. Your **first app is included**, and you do not need a credit card. After the trial, continue on **Starter** (\$6/month) or **Ultra Heavy** (\$30/month).

<Plans />

## How billing works

- The trial includes your **first connection**. You do not need a card to start.
- Connecting a **second app** during the trial starts checkout. Billing starts only when the trial ends, and you pay only for the time after it.
- After the trial, you need an active **Starter** or **Ultra Heavy** plan to keep your apps active. Without one, your apps are paused until you pick a plan.
- Paid plans are **flat**: connecting more apps never changes the price. The one-app limit applies only during the trial.

## Tool calls

| Plan | Successful tool calls |
|---|---|
| Free trial | 1,000 over the 7 days |
| Starter | 1,000 per calendar month (UTC), reset on the 1st |
| Ultra Heavy | 100,000 per calendar month (UTC) |

Only successful calls count. Failed calls show in **Logs**, but they do not use your allowance. If you often reach the Starter limit, move to Ultra Heavy. You can see your usage at any time in **Dashboard > Billing**.

## Data credit

[Public data apps](/integrations/public-data) (TikTok, Reddit, SEO, crypto, stock data and more) are paid per call from a separate **data credit** balance. They never use your tool calls.

| Source | Amount | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome credit | \$0.10, once | Never |
| Starter plan | \$1 every month | End of the month |
| Ultra Heavy plan | \$5 every month | End of the month |
| Top-up packs | \$20, \$50 or \$100 | Never |

A call uses the monthly plan credit first, then your own balance. Top up in **Dashboard > Billing**, or click the **Data credit** balance in the sidebar. Prices, refunds and the math: [Public data apps](/integrations/public-data#how-the-math-works).

## The Billing page

**Dashboard > Billing** shows:

- Your plan, and whether your access is active
- Your trial status and the days left, if you are on the trial
- Tool-call usage for this period (`used / limit`)
- Your data credit balance, with top-up packs
- Your renewal or cancellation date
- **Upgrade** or **Manage subscription**

If you come back from checkout and still see the trial for a moment, wait a minute and refresh. The plan syncs when the payment completes.

Already on Starter? Upgrade to Ultra Heavy from the same page. Your current subscription changes in place, so you are never billed twice.

## Manage your subscription

On a paid plan, **Manage subscription** opens the Polar customer portal, where you can:

- Update your payment method
- See invoices and receipts
- Cancel the renewal

## Billing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start?">
    No. The 7-day free trial includes your first app, with no card. You enter payment details only when you connect a second app during the trial, or pick a paid plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after the trial ends?">
    Your apps are paused until you start a Starter or Ultra Heavy plan. Pick a plan in **Dashboard > Billing** to turn them back on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is billing based on how many apps I connect?">
    Only during the trial: the trial includes one app, and a second one starts checkout. On a paid plan the price is flat. Connect as many apps as you like.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do public data apps count toward my tool calls?">
    No. They are paid from your data credit. Your plan's monthly data credit is used first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage or cancel my subscription later?">
    Yes. On a paid plan, open **Dashboard > Billing** and click **Manage subscription** to open the Polar customer portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My account is older and shows a longer trial. Why?">
    The trial length changed over time. Older accounts keep the trial they started with. New accounts get the current 7-day trial.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
