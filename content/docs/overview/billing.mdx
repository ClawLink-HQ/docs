## How billing works

- New accounts get a **7-day free trial**. Your **first connection is included** during the trial — no card needed to start.
- Connecting a **second app** during the trial starts checkout. Billing doesn't begin until your trial ends, and you only pay for the time after it.
- After the trial ends, you need an active **Starter** or **Ultra Heavy** plan to keep your integrations active. Without one, integrations are paused until you pick a plan.
- Paid plans are **flat-rate**: once you're on Starter or Ultra Heavy, connecting more apps never changes the price. The one-app limit only applies during the trial.

## Fair-use tool calls

Successful tool executions are counted on a fair-use basis:

- **Trial** — 1,000 tool calls across the 7-day window.
- **Starter** — 1,000 tool calls per calendar month (UTC), resetting on the 1st.
- **Ultra Heavy** — unlimited.

If your workflow starts reaching Starter volumes regularly, that's the signal to move to Ultra Heavy. You can see your usage at any time in **Dashboard > Billing**.

<Note>
  Fair-use covers normal day-to-day agent workflows. If you start hitting volumes that match the Ultra Heavy use case, we'll reach out before throttling.
</Note>

## What you see on the Billing page

In **Dashboard > Billing**, ClawLink shows:

- Your current plan and whether access is active
- Your trial status and days remaining, if applicable
- Tool-call usage for the current period (`used / limit`, or "Unlimited" on Ultra Heavy)
- Your renewal or cancellation date when a paid subscription is active
- A direct upgrade or **Manage subscription** action

If you return from checkout and still see the free plan for a moment, wait a minute and refresh. Billing state is synced after checkout completes and the webhook lands.

## Upgrading to a paid plan

To upgrade:

1. Go to **Dashboard > Billing**
2. Click **Choose Starter** (or **Choose Ultra Heavy** for unlimited tool calls)
3. Complete checkout in Polar
4. Return to the dashboard and refresh if needed

Your paid plan becomes active as soon as checkout and billing sync finish.

## Managing your subscription

If you already have an active subscription, use **Manage subscription** on the Billing page to:

- Update your payment method
- View invoices and receipts
- Cancel renewal for a future billing period

ClawLink sends you to the Polar customer portal for those actions.

## Billing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start?">
    No. New accounts get a 7-day free trial with the first app included and no card required. You only enter payment details when you connect a second app during the trial or pick a paid plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after the trial ends?">
    Your integrations are paused until you start a Starter or Ultra Heavy plan. Pick a plan from **Dashboard > Billing** to turn them back on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is billing based on how many apps I connect?">
    Only during the trial: the trial includes your first app, and connecting a second one starts checkout. Once you're on a paid plan, pricing is flat — connect as many apps as you like without changing what you pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage or cancel my subscription later?">
    Yes. Once you're on a paid plan, open **Dashboard > Billing** and click **Manage subscription** to open the Polar customer portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My account was created a while ago and shows a longer trial. Why?">
    Trial length changed over time as the product evolved. Older accounts keep the trial window that was in effect when they signed up; new accounts start with the current 7-day trial.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
