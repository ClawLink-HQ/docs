Connect your CRM and sales tools so your agent can manage leads, update deals, and keep your pipeline moving.

## Common apps in this category

- Apollo
- HubSpot
- Salesforce
- Pipedrive
- Dynamics 365

## Typical tasks

- Researching leads
- Creating or updating contacts
- Moving deals through pipelines
- Syncing notes from email or calls
- Preparing account context for outbound work
