## Where the data comes from

A normal ClawLink integration signs in to **your** account on the provider and acts as you. A public data app does not touch your account at all. ClawLink buys each call from specialist data providers. They collect the data from the public web, by scraping the public page the way a logged-out visitor sees it, or from public data APIs. ClawLink picks the provider for each action, cleans up the result and returns it to your agent as structured data.

| Stock market | Marketstack | End of day; intraday prices are delayed |

That is why:

- **Nothing to connect.** There is no OAuth screen and no API key, because the request is not made as you.
- **You only get public data.** Private accounts, follower-only posts, DMs and anything behind a login are not reachable. LinkedIn shows a logged-out visitor much less than a signed-in member, so its profile action returns headline, location and follower count, not work history.
- **Read-only.** Posting, liking, following, replying or deleting needs your own account's permission. For that, connect the regular app (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X and more) from the catalog: see [Social media integrations](/integrations/social-media). The two kinds work side by side: read a competitor's posts with the data app, then publish your reply through your connected account.

<Note>
  Data comes from the public web and platforms change what they show. A field that exists today can be empty tomorrow, and a heavily requested profile can return a temporary error. ClawLink retries transient errors inside one charge; a call that still fails is refunded.
</Note>

## How the math works

Every action has a fixed price per call. The cost of a task is the price of each call your agent makes, added up:

```text
cost = price per call × number of calls
```

Only the calls to ClawLink cost credit. What your agent does with the result (summarize it, compare it, write a report) runs on your agent's own model and costs nothing here.

### Prices that grow with the request

Most actions cost the same every time. Some SEO and AI visibility actions have a base price plus a small amount per item, because the provider charges us per item:

- `keyword_overview` with 50 keywords: $0.024 base + 50 × $0.00024 = **$0.036**.
- `google_serp` with 100 results: $0.004 for the first 10 + 9 more pages of 10 × $0.003 = **$0.031**.
- `backlinks` with 100 backlinks: $0.048 base + 100 × $0.000072 = **$0.0552**.

Each of these actions has a maximum (usually 100 items), so one call can never cost more than its cap. The tables below list the base price and the per-item price.

### Charges and refunds

- **An empty result is still charged.** A search that finds nothing costs the same as one that finds ten results, because the provider charges us either way.
- **A failed call is refunded.** If the provider errors after retries, the charge is reversed.
- **No subscription minimum.** You pay only for the calls your agent makes.

### Which balance pays

Credit is separate from your plan: data calls never count against monthly tool calls. A call takes credit in this order:

1. **Plan credit.** Starter includes $1 and Ultra Heavy includes $5 of credit every month. It resets each month and does not roll over.
2. **Your credit balance.** The $0.10 welcome credit plus any packs you buy. It does not expire.

Top up in **Dashboard > Billing** with a $20, $50 or $100 pack. You can see every charge and refund in **Dashboard > Logs**.

## How to prompt

Name the app id (`tiktok-data`, `instagram-data`, and so on) when you want to be sure the agent uses public data rather than a connected account. Handles go without the `@`. Posts, videos and pages take their full public URL. Lists come in pages: the result carries a cursor, and asking for "the next page" makes the agent pass it back.

```text
Using instagram-data, compare @nike and @adidas: follower counts and the engagement on their last 5 posts each.
```

```text
Using youtube-data, get the transcript of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=… and give me the five main points.
```

```text
Using ai-visibility-data, ask ChatGPT "best way to connect OpenClaw to Gmail" and list the sources it cites.
```

## Limits and good practice

- **Public only.** These apps never see private content, and they never act on a platform. Use them for research, monitoring and reporting.
- **Respect the platforms.** The data belongs to the people who posted it. Do not use these actions to build datasets of personal information, and follow each platform's terms for anything you publish from what you read.
- **Loops cost money.** An agent that polls a profile every minute spends credit every minute. Ask for what you need once, and keep an eye on the balance in the sidebar.
- **Not financial advice.** Market data is for research. Stock prices are end of day and intraday prices are delayed, so do not trade on them as live quotes.
- **Prices on this page are current at the time of writing.** Your agent sees each action's price in its description, and **Dashboard > Logs** shows the exact charge of every call.
