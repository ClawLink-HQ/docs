Public data apps are the group marked **Included with ClawLink** on your Connections page. Each one is named "<platform> public data" and is a different thing from the connectable app of the same platform: **Instagram public data** reads public Instagram, **Instagram** posts to your own account. They read what anyone can see on a platform without logging in: profiles, posts, comments, search results, trends. There is nothing to connect. Every account has them from the first minute, and every call is paid from your credit balance instead of your plan.

## How they work

A normal ClawLink integration signs in to **your** account on the provider and acts as you. A public data app does not touch your account at all. ClawLink fetches the public page or endpoint through its own data providers, the same way a logged-out visitor would, and returns the structured result to your agent.

That is why:

- **Nothing to connect.** There is no OAuth screen and no API key, because the request is not made as you.
- **You only get public data.** Private accounts, follower-only posts, DMs and anything behind a login are not reachable. LinkedIn shows a logged-out visitor much less than a signed-in member, so its profile action returns headline, location and follower count, not work history.
- **Read-only.** Posting, liking, following, replying or deleting needs your own account's permission. For that, connect the regular app (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X and more) from the catalog: see [Social media integrations](/integrations/social-media). The two kinds work side by side: read a competitor's posts with the data app, then publish your reply through your connected account.

<Note>
  Data comes from the public web and platforms change what they show. A field that exists today can be empty tomorrow, and a heavily requested profile can return a temporary error. ClawLink retries transient errors inside one charge; a call that still fails is refunded.
</Note>

## What a call costs

Each action has its own price, from **$0.002** for a TikTok or X read to **$0.10** for a LinkedIn post list. The price is written in every action's description, so your agent sees it before calling. Two rules:

- **An empty result is still charged.** A search that finds nothing costs the same as one that finds ten results, because the provider charges us either way.
- **A failed call is refunded.** If the provider errors after retries, the charge is reversed.

Credit is separate from your plan: data calls never count against monthly tool calls. New accounts start with **$0.10 of free credit**. Top up in **Dashboard > Billing** with a $20, $50 or $100 pack. You can see every charge and refund in **Dashboard > Logs**.

## How to prompt

Name the app id (`tiktok-data`, `instagram-data`, and so on) when you want to be sure the agent uses public data rather than a connected account. Handles go without the `@`. Posts, videos and pages take their full public URL. Lists come in pages: the result carries a cursor, and asking for "the next page" makes the agent pass it back.

```text
Using instagram-data, compare @nike and @adidas: follower counts and the engagement on their last 5 posts each.
```

```text
Using youtube-data, get the transcript of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=… and give me the five main points.
```

```text
Using facebook-data, search the Meta Ad Library for active ads mentioning "meal kit" in the US and summarize the offers.
```

## Limits and good practice

- **Public only.** These apps never see private content, and they never act on a platform. Use them for research, monitoring and reporting.
- **Respect the platforms.** The data belongs to the people who posted it. Do not use these actions to build datasets of personal information, and follow each platform's terms for anything you publish from what you read.
- **Loops cost money.** An agent that polls a profile every minute spends credit every minute. Ask for what you need once, and keep an eye on the balance in the sidebar.
- **Prices in this page are current at the time of writing.** The action description your agent reads is the authoritative price.
