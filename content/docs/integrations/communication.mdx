Connect your messaging and email services so your agent can send, read, and manage communications on your behalf.

## Common apps in this category

- Gmail
- Slack
- Outlook
- Discord
- Telegram
- Microsoft Teams
- Twilio
- Google Chat

## Typical tasks

- Drafting and sending emails
- Searching inboxes and summarizing threads
- Posting Slack or Discord updates
- Sending Telegram notifications
- Pulling chat context into another workflow
