## X is paid per call

X charges for every API call, so the **X** app is paid from your [data credit](/billing#data-credit), not from your plan's tool calls:

| Action | Price |
|---|---|
| Post, reply, like, repost, follow or DM | \$0.01875 |
| Post with a link | \$0.25 |
| Read posts | \$0.00625 per post returned |

A read is charged first for the page size your agent asks for (10 posts by default, 25 at most). ClawLink then refunds the posts X did not return.

<Tip>
  To read **other people's** public posts, profiles or trends, you do not need to connect anything. Use the [public data apps](/integrations/public-data), like TikTok, Instagram, X and Reddit public data.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Social platforms often change their scopes and posting rules. Check the current connect flow in the dashboard before you plan a workflow around an app.
</Note>
